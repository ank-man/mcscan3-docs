    One command: the pipeline¶

    mcscan3 pipeline run takes a directory of genomes and runs a full comparative analysis, resuming where it stopped if interrupted, and writes an HTML report.

    mcscan3 pipeline inputs genomes/                          # what was found, before running
mcscan3 pipeline run genomes/ -o results --ref grape --cpus 16 --ks
open results/report.html

    Inputs¶

    Put one or more files per genome in a directory, named <genome>.<anything>:

    Files Mode What happens
    grape.gff3.gz + grape.dna.fa.gz genome longest coding transcript per gene, CDS spliced and translated (prep genome)
    grape.gff3.gz + grape.cds.fa.gz [+ grape.pep.fa.gz] gff+cds longest transcript per gene; the GFF attribute matching the FASTA names is found automatically
    grape.bed + grape.cds / grape.pep processed used as given; the BED may be in MCScanX layout

    FASTA files are classified by their names (cds, pep, protein, dna, genome, ...) or, if the name does not tell, by their content. A sample sheet (--samples, columns species, gff, genome, bed, cds, pep) overrides the detection. Protein records are matched to the genes through their headers (transcript:, gene:, .CDS / -P suffixes); when they do not match, proteins are translated from the CDS.

    Steps¶

    1. inputs: data/<genome>.bed/.cds/.pep.
    2. orthology: OrthoFinder when installed (--orthofinder auto, the default; yes to require it): orthogroups, orthologues, gene trees and a rooted species tree. Otherwise (no) MCscan3's own: DIAMOND all-against-all, MCL orthogroups, reciprocal best hits.
    3. synteny: ortholog run for every pair of genomes and each genome against itself.
    4. soi: the Orthology Index of every block; soi/<a>.<b>.soi.anchors keep orthologous synteny (--soi-cutoff).
    5. pan: pan-genes linked by anchors that are orthologous synteny and in one orthogroup, completed with the other members of their orthogroups (<genome>_nonsyntenic columns), and a riparian plot.
    6. network: synteny network, clusters, profiles and a network species tree; the trees are compared and disagreements reported.
    7. ancestral: ancestral gene orders at every node of the species tree, ancestral chromosomes painted on every genome, and all karyotypes on the tree.
    8. dup: duplicate-gene classes of every genome.
    9. wgd (--ks): anchor-pair Ks (MCscan3's LPB93 estimator: no PAML needed), block-median Ks and mixture models per genome.
    10. wgd-consensus (--ks): WGDs placed on the species tree by consensus of four lines of evidence (WGD guide).
    11. report: report.html.

    The species tree matters

    Ancestral genomes and WGD placement depend on the rooted species tree. With few genomes, or genomes with WGDs, OrthoFinder's and the synteny-network trees can both be wrong (on six eudicots neither matched the accepted tree). The report flags disagreements; give a trusted tree with --tree species.nwk when it matters.

    Outputs¶

    results/
  run.json            what ran: status, time and notes of every step, and the results shown in the report
  samples.tsv         what was found
  data/               prepared <genome>.bed/.cds/.pep
  orthology/          OrthoFinder results or MCscan3's own orthogroups
  synteny/            ortholog run per pair (.last, .anchors, .lifted.anchors, dot plots)
  soi/                Orthology Index per block, .soi.anchors
  pan/                pangenes.tsv, riparian.png
  network/            net.edges/.clusters/.profile.tsv, net.tree.nwk
  ancestral/          anc.<node>.cars.tsv, <node>.karyotype.png, tree.karyotype.png
  dup/                <genome>.<class>.pairs, <genome>.genes
  wgd/                <genome>.anchor.ksd.tsv, .blocks.tsv, .blocks.png, consensus.tsv/.png
  report_files/       figures and interactive dot plot data used by the report
  report.html

    Steps that fail are listed in the report and in a table printed at the end of the run; the others still run. Re-running the same command skips finished work.

    Re-running some steps¶

    --steps re-runs only the steps named; the results of the others are kept from run.json:

    mcscan3 pipeline run genomes/ -o results --steps ancestral,report --tree species.nwk
mcscan3 pipeline report results          # only rebuild report.html (e.g. after an update)

    The report¶

    report.html is one self-contained page (no internet access needed; share the whole results/ directory or just report.html with report_files/). It has:

    • summary cards: genomes, genes, orthogroups, orthologous blocks, pan-genes, ancestral CARs and WGDs placed;
    • interactive dot plots of every genome pair and every genome against itself: pick a pair, scroll to zoom, drag to pan, click a chromosome name to zoom to it, hover a dot for its two genes, block, orientation and Orthology Index; colour by orientation, block or Orthology Index; download the view as PNG;
    • pan-genes (core, shell, private), riparian plot, synteny network and species trees, ancestral karyotypes on the tree, duplicate-gene classes as stacked bars, Ks mixtures and WGDs on the species tree;
    • the run: every step with its status and time, and every note.

    Figures open full size on click and tables sort by clicking a column header. The page follows the system's light or dark mode, with a toggle.

    Run time¶

    Six plant genomes (27,000-56,000 genes each) on 10 CPUs: OrthoFinder is the longest step (hours on a laptop; much less on a server); synteny for 21 pairs takes about 30 minutes; the other steps minutes each; --ks adds anchor Ks of every genome (native LPB93: minutes) and the WGD consensus.
