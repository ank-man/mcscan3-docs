    Demo atlas¶

    Every MCscan3 command, run on published genomes. Each card opens the run: the command line, its console output, the files it wrote and the figures it drew.

    224commands run
    145figures
    40groups
    18.8 htotal run time
    Synteny3.2 s

    synteny decay

    Decay of synteny with divergence: block N50 and coverage against anchor identity, for many genome pairs
    Synteny0.7 s

    synteny depth

    Synteny depth of each genome in a pairwise comparison
    Orthology1.6 min

    ortholog hogs

    Hierarchical orthologous groups at every node of a species tree, guided by synteny
    Orthology3.0 s

    ortholog qc

    Orthogroup quality: assignment, species overlap, sizes, domain homogeneity and dispersal, genomes, agreement
    Orthology57 s

    ortholog run

    One-command pipeline: align, filter, scan, liftover (MCscan tutorial)
    Ks0.8 s

    ks multireport

    Plot several Ks distributions in one figure
    WGD0.4 s

    wgd blocks

    Median Ks of each syntenic block (segment-guided Ks)
    WGD34 s

    wgd compare

    Ks distributions of several genomes or transcriptomes as ridgelines, with their WGD peaks
    WGD44 s

    wgd consensus

    WGDs on a species tree by consensus of block ratio, quota ratio, Ks and gene trees
    WGD29.5 min

    wgd mix

    Mixture models of a Ks distribution: GMM, ELMM, Bayesian GMM (BIC/AIC)
    WGD4.8 s

    wgd peaks

    Peaks of a Ks distribution (kernel density, bootstrap intervals)
    WGD6.2 s

    wgd ploidy

    Copies made by the last WGD (tetraploidy, hexaploidy, more) from syntenic depth against a reference
    WGD2.6 min

    wgd transcriptome

    WGD signatures from a transcriptome assembly (no genome): ORFs, paranome Ks and mixture models
    WGD19 s

    wgd tree-map

    Place gene duplications on a species tree by reconciling gene trees
    Ancestral genomes3.8 s

    ancestral adjacencies

    Ancestral gene adjacencies and CARs over HOGs, dated on the species tree
    Ancestral genomes6.3 s

    ancestral karyotype

    Group CARs into ancestral chromosomes and paint them on extant karyotypes
    Ancestral genomes3.3 s

    ancestral paint

    Paint extant chromosomes by the ancestral regions (CARs) they came from
    Ancestral genomes20 s

    ancestral tree-karyotype

    Species tree with a karyotype on every node, and fusion/fission/translocation counts per branch
    Macrosynteny9.5 s

    macro algs

    Ancestral linkage groups found from many genomes (or given), with their chromosomes
    Macrosynteny0.9 s

    macro plot

    Oxford dot plot of two genomes (ordered by enrichment) or ALG painting of many genomes
    Macrosynteny0.6 s

    macro test

    Chromosome pairs of two genomes sharing more gene families than chance (Fisher, FDR)
    Microsynteny3.6 s

    micro coexpr

    Co-expression of microsynteny block genes against shuffled and random blocks; tau, condition bias, gaps
    Microsynteny12 s

    micro density

    Syntenic density of the blocks per genome, false blocks from shuffled genomes, transitions on the tree
    Microsynteny0.5 s

    micro nodes

    Blocks dated on the species tree: ancestral, novel and lost at every node (Dollo)
    Pan-genome27 s

    pan cnv

    Local presence/absence and copy-number variation of pan-genes in windows along a reference, with hotspots
    Pan-genome3.6 h

    pan graph

    Graph pan-genome: graph built and genotyped by minigraph, bubbles, paths, growth, VCF, PLINK, diversity, regions
    Pan-genome1.1 min

    pan haplotype

    Haplotypes of regions across genomes from gene content, copy number, SVs and orientation, tested against traits
    Pan-genome1.7 min

    pan region

    Presence/absence and copy number of the pan-genes of regions (QTL, loci) traced into every genome
    Pan-genome24 s

    pan run

    Pan-genome two ways, syntenic pan-genes and k-mer sketches of assemblies: classes, curves, Heaps' law
