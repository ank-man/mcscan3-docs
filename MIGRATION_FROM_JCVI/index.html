    Migrating from JCVI¶

    MCscan3 contains the MCscan-related features of JCVI v1.6.7 (the version the outputs are verified against, see SOURCE_VERSION.md). Each JCVI command has one MCscan3 command, listed below.

    Running old command lines¶

    Put mcscan3 legacy in place of python -m jcvi.. The rest of the line stays the same:

    python -m jcvi.compara.catalog ortholog grape peach --no_strip_names   # JCVI
mcscan3 legacy compara.catalog ortholog grape peach --no_strip_names    # the same run in MCscan3
mcscan3 ortholog run grape peach --no-strip-names                       # the new spelling

    mcscan3 legacy prints the new command name, then runs it. The MCscan tutorial runs unchanged this way (tested in tests/test_tutorial.py, with outputs identical to JCVI's).

    Option spelling¶

    • Options are lower case with hyphens: --no_strip_names becomes --no-strip-names and --tandem_Nmax becomes --tandem-nmax. Legacy command lines are translated automatically.

    • Renamed options:

    • --theme is now --grid-color

    Outputs and behaviour¶

    Outputs are byte-identical to JCVI's for the same inputs. Figures are pixel-identical by default, and --theme mcscan3 selects the new house style. Every deliberate difference is listed in DEVIATIONS.md. The main ones are:

    • Commands that always crashed in JCVI v1.6.7 work. Their reference outputs are JCVI plus the minimal fixes in tools/jcvi_fixes.patch.
    • Silent JCVI bugs keep JCVI's behaviour by default and have opt-in fixes, such as ortholog omg-prepare --integer-weights and the --plain text histograms.
    • Reports go to stderr, as in JCVI; data goes to stdout or files.
    • Figure metadata says Creator: MCscan3 <version>.

    Dependencies and external programs¶

    MCscan3 needs neither JCVI nor its compiled extensions. seaborn and palettable are replaced by recorded values, R is replaced by matplotlib for plot histogram, and the gmm-bic program is replaced by a built-in mixture fit (ks report --fit). External aligners and tools (LAST, BLAST+, DIAMOND, bedtools, ClustalW, MUSCLE, PAL2NAL, PAML, Gblocks, PhyML, RAxML, EMBOSS PHYLIP, CLUSTER 3.0) are found on PATH, or in the directory named by MCSCAN3_<PROGRAM>_PATH (e.g. MCSCAN3_LASTAL_PATH). plot tree, phylo draw and the rooting and NJ steps of phylo build need the phylo extra (pip install mcscan3[phylo], which installs ete4).

    New in MCscan3¶

    These commands have no JCVI counterpart.

    MCscan3 command Description Method
    mcscan3 prep genome Longest-isoform BED, CDS and protein from a genome GFF3 and FASTA Longest coding transcript per gene, CDS spliced from the genome and translated.
    mcscan3 align mmseqs Search proteins or CDS with MMseqs2 (BLAST tabular output for synteny) Steinegger M, Söding J (2017) MMseqs2 enables sensitive protein sequence searching for the analysis of massive data sets. Nat Biotechnol 35:1026; run as an external tool.
    mcscan3 align many Search many genomes in one run (optionally on a GPU); one .last file per pair One combined database and one search for all genome pairs, as in all-against-all orthology searches (Emms & Kelly 2019, Genome Biol 20:238); GPU search with MMseqs2-GPU (Kallenborn et al. 2025, Nat Methods); MMseqs2 or DIAMOND run as external tools.
    mcscan3 ortholog hogs Hierarchical orthologous groups at every node of a species tree, guided by synteny Hierarchical orthologous groups after Altenhoff AM et al. (2013) Inferring hierarchical orthologous groups from orthologous gene pairs. PLoS One 8:e53786 and Majidian S et al. (2025) Orthology inference at scale with FastOMA. Nat Methods 22:269; synteny-weighted links; reimplemented.
    mcscan3 ks kaks Ka, Ks and Ka/Ks of gene pairs with MCscan3's own estimators (no PAML) Native NG86 (Nei & Gojobori 1986), LPB93 (Li 1993; Pamilo & Bianchi 1993) and GY94 maximum likelihood (Goldman & Yang 1994) on protein-guided codon alignments; in the spirit of KaKs_Calculator.
    mcscan3 ortholog soi Keep orthologous synteny: blocks whose anchors are mostly orthologs (Orthology Index) Zhang R-G, et al. (2025) SOI: robust identification of orthologous synteny with the Orthology Index and broad applications in evolutionary genomics. Nucleic Acids Research; reimplemented from the paper.
    mcscan3 dup classify Classify duplicated genes: WGD, tandem, proximal, transposed, dispersed, singleton Qiao X, et al. (2019) Gene duplication and evolution in recurring polyploidization-diploidization cycles in plants. Genome Biology 20:38 (DupGen_finder); reimplemented from the paper.
    mcscan3 wgd ksd Whole-paranome or anchor-pair Ks distribution with node weighting Zwaenepoel A, Van de Peer Y (2019) wgd - simple command line tools for the analysis of ancient whole-genome duplications. Bioinformatics 35:2153; Chen H, Zwaenepoel A, Van de Peer Y (2024) wgd v2. Bioinformatics 40:btae272; reimplemented from the papers.
    mcscan3 wgd peaks Peaks of a Ks distribution (kernel density, bootstrap intervals) Zwaenepoel A, Van de Peer Y (2019) wgd - simple command line tools for the analysis of ancient whole-genome duplications. Bioinformatics 35:2153; Chen H, Zwaenepoel A, Van de Peer Y (2024) wgd v2. Bioinformatics 40:btae272; reimplemented from the papers.
    mcscan3 wgd mix Mixture models of a Ks distribution: GMM, ELMM, Bayesian GMM (BIC/AIC) Zwaenepoel A, Van de Peer Y (2019) wgd - simple command line tools for the analysis of ancient whole-genome duplications. Bioinformatics 35:2153; Chen H, Zwaenepoel A, Van de Peer Y (2024) wgd v2. Bioinformatics 40:btae272; reimplemented from the papers.
    mcscan3 wgd blocks Median Ks of each syntenic block (segment-guided Ks) Zwaenepoel A, Van de Peer Y (2019) wgd - simple command line tools for the analysis of ancient whole-genome duplications. Bioinformatics 35:2153; Chen H, Zwaenepoel A, Van de Peer Y (2024) wgd v2. Bioinformatics 40:btae272; reimplemented from the papers.
    mcscan3 wgd correct Rate-correct a species-divergence Ks onto a focal species' scale Sensalari C, Maere S, Lohaus R (2022) ksrates: positioning whole-genome duplications relative to speciation events in KS distributions. Bioinformatics 38:530; relative rate test with an outgroup.
    mcscan3 wgd tree-map Place gene duplications on a species tree by reconciling gene trees Chen Z, et al. (2023) Tree2GD: a phylogenomic method to detect large-scale gene duplication events. Bioinformatics 39:btad669; LCA reconciliation reimplemented from the paper. --paralogs: McKain MR, et al. (2016) A phylogenomic assessment of ancient polyploidy and genome evolution across the Poales. Genome Biol Evol 8:1150-1164 (PUG), reimplemented from the paper.
    mcscan3 ortholog qc Orthogroup quality: assignment, species overlap, sizes, domain homogeneity and dispersal, genomes, agreement After cogeqc (Almeida-Silva F & Van de Peer Y, Bioconductor), reimplemented with MCscan3 scores: Sørensen-Dice homogeneity of domain sets against shuffled domains, dispersal normalised by domain abundance; OrthoFinder results (Emms DM & Kelly S (2019) Genome Biol 20:238) or MCscan3 HOGs; adjusted Rand index (Hubert L & Arabie P (1985) J Classif 2:193-218). Pfam search with HMMER hmmsearch.
    mcscan3 te ltr Intact LTR retrotransposons: TSDs, motifs, LTR identity, insertion ages, nested insertions Native k-mer seeded search for direct repeats with gapped X-drop extension, TG..CA motif and target-site duplication boundaries, banded LTR alignment and K2P ages, and iterative de-nesting; after the element structure used by LTRharvest (Ellinghaus D, et al. (2008) BMC Bioinformatics 9:18), LTR_FINDER (Xu Z & Wang H (2007) Nucleic Acids Res 35:W265) and LTR_retriever (Ou S & Jiang N (2018) Plant Physiol 176:1410), and nested-element dating after SanMiguel P, et al. (1998) Nat Genet 20:43; no code reused.
    mcscan3 te classify Clade-level TE classification from protein domains: lineage, Wicker code, domain order, strand, support Six-frame HMMER hmmsearch against REXdb (Neumann P, et al. (2019) Mob DNA 10:1) or GyDB domain profiles with score-weighted lineage descent and domain-order checks; after TEsorter (Zhang RG, et al. (2022) Hortic Res 9:uhac017); no code reused.
    mcscan3 te tir DNA transposons and MITEs from terminal inverted repeats: TSDs, Wicker superfamily, families, nesting Native reverse-complement k-mer seeds chained on the anti-diagonal with gapped X-drop extension, symmetric boundary search with target-site duplications, family support from shared ends at independent insertion sites and superfamily calls from TSD length and terminal motifs (Wicker T, et al. (2007) Nat Rev Genet 8:973); after TIR-Learner (Su W, et al. (2019) Mol Plant 12:447) and MITE-Hunter (Han Y & Wessler SR (2010) Nucleic Acids Res 38:e199); more copies found by aligning each family's consensus ends back to the genome; elements over 2 kb checked for transposase domains against REXdb (Neumann P, et al. (2019) Mob DNA 10:1); no code reused.
    mcscan3 te helitron Helitrons from 5'-TC and hairpin-CTRR-3' ends shared by copies at independent sites; families, nesting Native search for CTRR-T 3' ends keyed by the bases before them, shared by copies at independent insertion sites (edit distance of the flanks) with a hairpin before the end, then the A
    mcscan3 te library Non-redundant TE library from the finders and curated libraries, labelled for masking Sequences of te ltr, te tir, te helitron and curated libraries merged longest first; a sequence is dropped when a kept one of the same order holds at least 0.8^k of its sampled k-mers (about 80% identity over its length), after the 80-80-80 family rule of Wicker T, et al. (2007) Nat Rev Genet 8:973.
    mcscan3 te annotate Whole-genome TE annotation with a library: hits with Kimura divergence, composition, landscape RepeatMasker (Smit AFA, Hubley R & Green P, RepeatMasker Open-4.0) with the EDTA benchmark settings (Ou S, et al. (2019) Genome Biol 20:275): -q -no_is -norna -nolow -div 40 -cutoff 225; CpG-adjusted Kimura divergence per hit; composition counted on the union of the hits of each class.
    mcscan3 te lai LTR Assembly Index of the genome and along the chromosomes, with the identity correction After Ou S, Chen J & Jiang N (2018) Nucleic Acids Res 46:e126: raw LAI = 100 x intact LTR retrotransposon bp / LTR sequence bp; LAI = raw + 2.8138 x (94 - mean LTR identity); 3 Mb windows moved by 300 kb; LTR identity from each LTR sequence aligned to its best-covering copy of the same family; no code reused.
    mcscan3 te age Insertion history: ages per clade, nested chronology, solo:intact ratios, insertions through time Insertion ages T = K / 2r from the Kimura distance of the two LTRs (SanMiguel P, et al. (1998) Nat Genet 20:43; rate of Ma J & Bennetzen JL (2004) PNAS 101:12404); nested elements younger than their hosts; solo LTRs with the filter of Ou S & Jiang N (2018) Plant Physiol 176:1410 and solo:intact ratios after Vitte C & Panaud O (2003) Mol Biol Evol 20:528.
    mcscan3 te simulate Simulated genome with TEs at known places, nested insertions and decoys (truth table for benchmarks) Elements are inserted one after another into a random background, so later insertions land inside earlier ones; LTR pairs diverge with age (transition:transversion 2:1, short indels) and each insertion duplicates its 5 bp target site; decoys: solo LTRs, truncated copies, tandem duplications and satellites.
    mcscan3 phylo busco Phylogenomics from single-copy BUSCO genes: markers, alignments, supermatrix, ML, gene and ASTRAL trees, gCF After BUSCO_phylogenomics (McGowan J & Fitzpatrick DA, GitHub jamiemcg/BUSCO_phylogenomics) and BUSCO (Manni M, et al. (2021) Mol Biol Evol 38:4647-4654), reimplemented; lineage chosen from NCBI Taxonomy. Gene concordance factors after Minh BQ, et al. (2020) Mol Biol Evol 37:2727-2733. Uses HMMER, FAMSA/MAFFT/MUSCLE, IQ-TREE or FastTree and ASTRAL when installed.
    mcscan3 plot phylo Tree figures: rectangular or circular, supports and concordance pies, clade shading, aligned data, tanglegrams Native tree drawing in the MCscan3 house style (features as in ete3, Huerta-Cepas J, et al. (2016) Mol Biol Evol 33:1635-1638, and iTOL, Letunic I & Bork P (2024) Nucleic Acids Res 52:W78-W82).
    mcscan3 pan genes Synteny-constrained pan-gene sets across genomes, placed on a reference Lovell JT, et al. (2022) GENESPACE tracks regions of interest and gene copy number variation across multiple genomes. eLife 11:e78526; reimplemented from the paper with MCscan3's synteny.
    mcscan3 pan run Pan-genome two ways, syntenic pan-genes and k-mer sketches of assemblies: classes, curves, Heaps' law Gene-based pan-genes as in pan genes. Sequence-based: FracMinHash sketches (Irber L, et al. (2022) Lightweight compositional analysis of metagenomes with FracMinHash and minimum metagenome covers. bioRxiv 2022.01.11.475838); identity from Jaccard as in Mash (Ondov BD, et al. (2016) Genome Biol 17:132). Heaps' law for open and closed pan-genomes (Tettelin H, et al. (2008) Comparative genomics: the bacterial pan-genome. Curr Opin Microbiol 11:472-477). Reimplemented.
    mcscan3 synteny decay Decay of synteny with divergence: block N50 and coverage against anchor identity, for many genome pairs MCscan3: per genome pair, the median identity of anchor genes (from the alignment the anchors came from), number and N50 of blocks and the share of genes inside blocks, plotted against each other by group.
    mcscan3 plot riparian Riparian (braid) plot of synteny across several genomes Lovell JT, et al. (2022) GENESPACE tracks regions of interest and gene copy number variation across multiple genomes. eLife 11:e78526; reimplemented from the paper with MCscan3's synteny.
    mcscan3 plot dot3d Three-genome dot plot in 3D, with the pairwise dot plots on the walls of the cube MCscan3: genes syntenic in a reference and two other genomes placed in a cube of the three genome coordinates (base pairs), chromosomes ordered and flipped after the reference, pairwise anchors projected on the walls; static (matplotlib) and self-contained interactive HTML renderings.
    mcscan3 plot circos Circular synteny plot of several genomes, with gene density and ribbons coloured by the reference Krzywinski M, et al. (2009) Circos: an information aesthetic for comparative genomics. Genome Res 19:1639-1645; reimplemented in matplotlib: genome arcs, gene density track, Bezier ribbons for synteny blocks, reference colours carried through the blocks.
