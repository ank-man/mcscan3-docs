    mcscan3 te tir¶

    DNA transposons with terminal inverted repeats, and MITEs.

    Group te · status new · new in MCscan3

    Synopsis¶

    mcscan3 te tir [OPTIONS] GENOME

    Description¶

    New in MCscan3: a native, parallel structural search written from the structure of the elements (Wicker T et al. 2007, Nat Rev Genet 8:973) and the superfamily rules used by TIR-Learner (Su W, Gu X & Peterson T 2019, Mol Plant 12:447) and MITE-Hunter (Han Y & Wessler SR 2010, NAR 38:e199), without their code:

    1. seeds: every k-mer of a chromosome chunk is paired with the reverse complement of the k-mers up to --max-len downstream (low-complexity and tandem k-mers left out);
    2. inverted repeats: seed pairs on one anti-diagonal (x + y constant, indels allowed) are chained, then extended outward and inward by a gapped X-drop, so each arm is a TIR;
    3. boundaries: the two ends are moved together to the best target-site duplication (TSD), so chance base pairs beyond the TIRs (the palindromic TA of Tc1-Mariner) are trimmed;
    4. filters: TIR length and identity (banded alignment of one TIR against the reverse complement of the other), element length, N content, tandem TIRs;
    5. superfamily: from the TSD and the ends, after Wicker et al.: TA (Tc1-Mariner, DTT), TTAA (PiggyBac, DTB), 3 bp TWA (PIF-Harbinger, DTH), 3 bp with 5'-CACT (CACTA, DTC), 5 bp (Transib, DTR), 8 bp (hAT, DTA), 9-11 bp (Mutator, DTM);
    6. families: elements sharing k-mers of their TIRs are one family; the superfamily of a family is the majority call of its copies, and --min-copies removes single copies (most false structural calls are single); copies up to --mite-max bp are MITEs.
    7. family ends: the 50 bp ends of each family are aligned back to the genome (11-mer seeds, banded alignment, identity 0.8 or more); a start and an end at the family's length with a TSD of its superfamily is another copy;
    8. domains: with --domains, elements over 2 kb are searched for protein domains (REXdb); elements with retrotransposon or Helitron domains are dropped, a transposase sets the superfamily, and long elements without one are dropped unless CACTA or Mutator.

    Writes <prefix>.tir.tsv, <prefix>.tir.gff3, <prefix>.tir.fa and <prefix>.tirlib.fa (the longest copy of each family) and a figure.

    Arguments¶

    Argument Description
    GENOME Genome FASTA (plain or gzipped).

    Options¶

    Option Description
    --prefix, -o Output prefix.
    --cpus Parallel processes (chromosome chunks). (default: 4)
    --min-tir Shortest TIR (bp). (default: 10)
    --max-tir Longest TIR (bp). (default: 1000)
    --min-len Shortest element (bp). (default: 50)
    --max-len Longest element (bp). (default: 20000)
    --mite-max Elements up to this length are MITEs. (default: 800)
    --min-identity Least identity of the two TIRs (0-1). (default: 0.8)
    --min-copies Least copies of a family (1 keeps single copies). (default: 2)
    --no-tsd Keep elements without a target-site duplication.
    --k Seed k-mer length. (default: 11)
    --domains Protein domains of elements over 2 kb (hmmsearch): rexdb, rexdb-plant, rexdb-metazoa, an HMM file, none, or auto (rexdb when hmmsearch is installed). (default: auto)
    --format Image format. (default: pdf)
    --dpi Dots per inch. (default: 300)

    Worked example¶

    Run on real data in the MCscan3 showcase (all commands on published genomes, in workflow order). The command line, console output, output files and figures below are those of that run.

    TIR DNA transposons and MITEs of rice chromosome 1, with superfamilies from TSD length and terminal motifs.

    mcscan3 te tir $DC/runs/te_rice/Chr1.fa --cpus $CPU -o te/Chr1 && head -3 te/Chr1.tir.tsv

    run time 2 min 5 s · data real · showcase step te_tir

    Variables: $DC the data cache; $CPU threads.

    Method and references¶

    New in MCscan3 (no JCVI equivalent). Native reverse-complement k-mer seeds chained on the anti-diagonal with gapped X-drop extension, symmetric boundary search with target-site duplications, family support from shared ends at independent insertion sites and superfamily calls from TSD length and terminal motifs (Wicker T, et al. (2007) Nat Rev Genet 8:973); after TIR-Learner (Su W, et al. (2019) Mol Plant 12:447) and MITE-Hunter (Han Y & Wessler SR (2010) Nucleic Acids Res 38:e199); more copies found by aligning each family's consensus ends back to the genome; elements over 2 kb checked for transposase domains against REXdb (Neumann P, et al. (2019) Mob DNA 10:1); no code reused.
