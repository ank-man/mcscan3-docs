    mcscan3 macro algs¶

    Ancestral linkage groups from many genomes.

    Group macro · status new · new in MCscan3

    Synopsis¶

    mcscan3 macro algs [OPTIONS] BEDS...

    Description¶

    New in MCscan3. Ancestral linkage groups (ALGs) are sets of gene families that stayed together on one chromosome since a distant ancestor, e.g. the 24-29 bilaterian ALGs (Simakov et al. 2022, Science Advances 8:eabi5884) or the 17 ALGs of the metazoan ancestor. Here they are found without a reference. Each pair of families is weighted by the share of the genomes having both in which they sit on one chromosome; the families are then split into groups whose pairs share a chromosome in more than --resolution (default 0.4) of the genomes on average: the Constant Potts Model (Traag et al. 2011, Phys Rev E 84:016114) optimised by Leiden when igraph is installed, else MCL. Pairs below --min-link (default 0.2) are left out of the graph, for speed. Families are used when present in at least --min-genomes genomes (default: half, at least two); groups of fewer than --min-size families are dropped. Fusions in a few lineages do not merge two ALGs (they are on one chromosome in a minority of genomes), and fissions in a few do not split one. With a rooted species tree (--tree), genomes are weighted so that each clade below a node has an equal share: a rearrangement shared by a large, densely sampled clade then counts once against its sister lineage, and the outgroups decide what was ancestral (a tie is not a link).

    With --reference, each ALG is named after the chromosome of that genome holding most of its families (ref:chr, letters when several ALGs share one), else ALG01, ALG02, ... by size. ALGs that are already known (e.g. the bilaterian ALGs on amphioxus genes) are given with --known gene<TAB>ALG; families then take the ALG of most of their genes in that table:

    mcscan3 macro algs *.bed --families Orthogroups.tsv -o metazoa --reference amphioxus
mcscan3 macro algs *.bed --families Orthogroups.tsv -o metazoa --known bfl_blg.tsv

    Writes <prefix>.algs.tsv (family, ALG) and <prefix>.alg_summary.tsv (per ALG: families and, per genome, the chromosome holding most of them and its share).

    Arguments¶

    Argument Description
    BEDS Genes of each genome (BED).

    Options¶

    Option Description
    --prefix, -o Output prefix.
    --families Orthogroups.tsv or genefamily table.
    --known Known ALGs as geneALG (instead of finding them).
    --tree Rooted species tree: weight genomes by clade.
    --reference Name ALGs after this genome's chromosomes.
    --resolution Least mean co-location within an ALG. (default: 0.4)
    --min-link Family pairs co-located less are ignored. (default: 0.2)
    --min-genomes Families in fewer genomes are left out (default: half).
    --min-size Smallest ALG (families). (default: 10)
    --seed Random seed (Leiden). (default: 666)
    --min-genes Smallest chromosome kept (genes with a family). (default: 20)
    --max-copies Families with more genes in a genome are left out there. (default: 4)

    Worked example¶

    Run on real data in the MCscan3 showcase (all commands on published genomes, in workflow order). The command line, console output, output files and figures below are those of that run.

    Ancestral linkage groups found de novo and from the grape karyotype.

    mcscan3 macro algs $R/data/amborella.bed $R/data/grape.bed $R/data/ath.bed $R/data/poplar.bed $R/data/peach.bed $R/data/soybean.bed --families $R/orthology/orthofinder/Results_mcscan3/Orthogroups/Orthogroups.tsv --tree $R/ancestral/species.nwk --reference grape -o macro/denovo  && mcscan3 macro algs $R/data/grape.bed $R/data/ath.bed $R/data/poplar.bed $R/data/peach.bed $R/data/soybean.bed --families $R/orthology/orthofinder/Results_mcscan3/Orthogroups/Orthogroups.tsv --known karyotype/grape.labels.tsv -o macro/eudicots && head -3 macro/eudicots.algs.tsv

    run time 9.5 s · data real · showcase step macro_algs

    Variables: $R the eudicot pipeline run (six genomes).

    Method and references¶

    New in MCscan3 (no JCVI equivalent). Macrosynteny after Simakov O et al. (2020) Deeply conserved synteny resolves early events in vertebrate evolution. Nat Ecol Evol 4:820; Simakov O et al. (2022) Deeply conserved synteny and the evolution of metazoan chromosomes. Sci Adv 8:eabi5884; Schultz DT et al. (2023) Ancient gene linkages support ctenophores as sister to other animals. Nature 618:110; one-sided Fisher exact tests with Benjamini-Hochberg FDR; reimplemented. ALGs by the Constant Potts Model (Traag VA, Van Dooren P, Nesterov Y 2011, Phys Rev E 84:016114) on family co-location, optimised by Leiden (Traag et al. 2019, Sci Rep 9:5233).
