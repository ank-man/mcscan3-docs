    mcscan3 ortholog qc¶

    Quality of orthogroups, genomes and annotations.

    Group ortholog · status new · new in MCscan3

    Synopsis¶

    mcscan3 ortholog qc [OPTIONS]

    Description¶

    New in MCscan3, after the orthogroup and genome checks of cogeqc (Almeida-Silva F & Van de Peer Y, Bioconductor), reimplemented with its own scores. Reads orthogroups from:

    • an OrthoFinder results folder (Orthogroups/Orthogroups.tsv, unassigned genes, duplications per species tree node, the rooted species tree), or Orthogroups.tsv / N0.tsv alone;
    • MCscan3 HOGs (ortholog hogs: <prefix>.genes.tsv; every gene in its topmost HOG, or the HOGs at --node);
    • a long table (group, species, gene) or one group per line (genes, with --pep/--bed for their species).

    and reports, for every species: genes, genes in orthogroups, unassigned genes, orthogroups with the species, species-specific orthogroups and their genes, single- and multi-copy orthogroups, duplications (OrthoFinder); orthogroups shared by every pair of species; orthogroup sizes and copy numbers. With domains (--domains, any of the formats of mcscan3.core.domains, or --pfam Pfam-A.hmm to search the proteomes) every orthogroup gets:

    • homogeneity H: the mean Sørensen-Dice similarity 2|A∩B|/(|A|+|B|) of the domain sets of all pairs of its annotated genes (genes without domains are left out; up to --max-size genes, sampled beyond). H is also given within each species, and for orthogroups of the same sizes with the domain sets shuffled among genes (what chance gives);
    • dispersal D: for each domain, (groups holding it − 1) / (genes with it − 1), 0 when all its genes are in one orthogroup and 1 when every gene is alone; D of an orthogroup is the mean over its domains. Unassigned genes count as groups of one, so domains split by under-clustering raise D;
    • score H − D: high for orthogroups that are both internally consistent and not split.

    With --busco (BUSCO or phylo busco runs) and --genome (assemblies) the genome table adds completeness, assembly length, N50/L50 of scaffolds and contigs, GC and gaps. --compare measures agreement with a second grouping (orthogroups kept exactly, split, merged; best Jaccard; adjusted Rand index):

    mcscan3 ortholog qc --orthofinder Results_Oct26 --domains pfam.domtbl --busco ath=busco_ath -o qc

    Arguments¶

    Argument Description

    Options¶

    Option Description
    --prefix, -o Output prefix.
    --orthofinder OrthoFinder results folder (or Orthogroups.tsv).
    --groups Orthogroups: MCscan3 HOG genes.tsv, a group/species/gene table or a group list.
    --node Node of the HOGs to use (default: the root).
    --pep Proteome NAME=FASTA (gene species, protein stats; repeatable). (default: [])
    --bed Annotation NAME=BED (gene species and counts; repeatable). (default: [])
    --domains Domains: gene/domain table, HMMER domtblout, pfam_scan or InterProScan (repeatable). (default: [])
    --pfam Pfam-A.hmm: search the --pep proteomes with hmmsearch.
    --evalue Largest domain E-value kept. (default: 1e-05)
    --max-size Annotated genes per orthogroup scored (sampled beyond). (default: 200)
    --busco BUSCO run NAME=DIR or a phylo busco status table (repeatable). (default: [])
    --genome Assembly NAME=FASTA for N50 and the like (repeatable). (default: [])
    --compare A second grouping to compare with (any --groups format).
    --compare-node Node of HOGs given to --compare.
    --tree Species tree (Newick) for the figures.
    --cpus Threads for hmmsearch. (default: 4)
    --seed Seed for sampling and shuffling. (default: 1)
    --format Image format. (default: pdf)
    --dpi Dots per inch. (default: 300)

    Worked example¶

    Run on real data in the MCscan3 showcase (all commands on published genomes, in workflow order). The command line, console output, output files and figures below are those of that run.

    Quality of the orthogroups of six eudicots: genes assigned per species, orthogroup sizes, single-copy and species-specific groups, and duplications placed on the species tree.

    mkdir -p ortholog && mcscan3 ortholog qc --orthofinder $R/orthology/orthofinder/Results_mcscan3 \
 --pep amborella=$R/data/amborella.pep --pep grape=$R/data/grape.pep --pep ath=$R/data/ath.pep \
 --pep poplar=$R/data/poplar.pep --pep peach=$R/data/peach.pep --pep soybean=$R/data/soybean.pep \
 --tree $DC/runs/eudicots_species.nwk -o ortholog/eudicots.qc && cat ortholog/eudicots.qc.species.tsv

    run time 3.0 s · data real · showcase step ortholog_qc

    Variables: $R the eudicot pipeline run (six genomes); $DC the data cache.

    Method and references¶

    New in MCscan3 (no JCVI equivalent). After cogeqc (Almeida-Silva F & Van de Peer Y, Bioconductor), reimplemented with MCscan3 scores: Sørensen-Dice homogeneity of domain sets against shuffled domains, dispersal normalised by domain abundance; OrthoFinder results (Emms DM & Kelly S (2019) Genome Biol 20:238) or MCscan3 HOGs; adjusted Rand index (Hubert L & Arabie P (1985) J Classif 2:193-218). Pfam search with HMMER hmmsearch.
