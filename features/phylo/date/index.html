    mcscan3 phylo date¶

    Divergence times on a species tree, with the checks that show how far to trust each age.

    Group phylo · status new · new in MCscan3

    Synopsis¶

    mcscan3 phylo date [OPTIONS]

    Description¶

    New in MCscan3. phylo date turns a species tree into a time tree. The tree and the loci come from one of three places:

    • --from-busco PREFIX: a phylo busco run. Its species tree (ASTRAL, else the concatenated tree), its trimmed single-copy alignments and its gene trees are reused.
    • --from-orthofinder DIR: an OrthoFinder results folder. The rooted species tree is dated, and the single-copy orthogroups are aligned and trimmed here.
    • --alignments: any set of aligned loci, with --tree. To date the topology of a synteny network tree (network tree), give --from-network PREFIX. Its branch lengths measure synteny, not substitutions, so only the topology is used, and the loci supply the sequence data.

    The tree must be rooted (or rooted here with --outgroup) and fully resolved.

    Calibrations¶

    Calibrations come from two places, and the two are combined.

    MCscan3's calibration library (--library, on by default) stores node ages for the main clades with fossil records or well-supported secondary ages: vertebrates, eudicots, Vitaceae, filamentous ascomycetes, dinoflagellates, Haemosporida and the apicomplexan-dinoflagellate split. Each comes with its source and a confidence level. Each node is defined by two NCBI Taxonomy taxa (crown mammals are the most recent common ancestor of Monotremata and Theria). A library age is applied only when the tree has tips on both sides, so it always lands on the node it was meant for.

    • Tip lineages. The NCBI lineage of every tip is looked up from --species TIP=SPECIES, a fetch manifest (--manifest), or the tip name itself (plasmodium_falciparum_3d7 is looked up as Plasmodium falciparum 3d7, then Plasmodium falciparum, then Plasmodium). The lineages are saved as OUT.taxonomy.tsv. --taxonomy FILE gives them directly, with no lookup.
    • Confidence. --library-confidence (low, medium or high; default medium) sets the least confidence used. Rows marked low are secondary ages or fossils whose placement is debated, and they need --library-confidence low.
    • Checking the library. The library is at src/mcscan3/features/phylo/calibrations.tsv. Check every row you rely on against its source before publication.

    Your own table (--calibrations) is tab-separated, with ages in million years before present:

    scheme   name           taxa                                   min    max    source
*        root           root                                   650    1100   apicomplexan-dinoflagellate split, Strassert et al. 2021
fossil   Dinophyceae    taxon:Gonyaulacales|Suessiales         240           Middle Triassic dinoflagellate cysts
fossil   Haemosporida   taxon:Haemosporida                     100           Paleohaemoproteus in Burmese amber
host     Mammal-Plasmodium  ~^plasmodium_(vivax|knowlesi|cynomolgi|berghei)  64  120  host co-divergence
    • taxa names the node in one of four ways:
      • taxon:A|B is the most recent common ancestor of the tips in NCBI taxa A and B.
      • taxon:A is the crown of taxon A. It is used only if A is monophyletic in the tree; otherwise it is skipped and the reason is logged, because an age placed on a polyphyletic group would date the wrong node.
      • Two or more tip names give their most recent common ancestor. root is the root.
      • A regular expression after ~ selects tips by name.
    • Minimum and maximum. A minimum without a maximum is a fossil-style lower bound, a truncated Cauchy with a long tail toward older ages. A maximum alone is an upper bound. Both together are soft bounds, with 2.5% of the probability outside each end.
    • Schemes. Rows with the same scheme are analysed together, and rows of scheme * belong to every scheme. Put independent lines of evidence in separate schemes (fossils, secondary ages from a larger dated tree, host co-divergence, mutation rates). Agreement between schemes is the strongest evidence that an age is right. The library's schemes are fossil and secondary.
    • Your rows win. A row of yours replaces a library row of the same name, and on a node that both calibrate, your row is used.
    • The root needs a maximum in every scheme. It can come from a library node that spans the whole tree, a root row, or --root-max.

    OUT.calibrations.tsv lists every calibration considered: where it came from, the node and its number of tips, its bounds, and whether it was used, replaced or skipped, with the reason.

    Method¶

    1. Clock-like loci.
      • Each locus is scored by how well its gene tree agrees with the species tree, then by the spread of its root-to-tip distances.
      • The best --loci loci are kept.
      • They are grouped by rate into --rate-partitions partitions.
    2. MCMCTree, with the approximate likelihood (gradient and Hessian of the branch lengths under LG+G4 by codeml).
      • The other nodes get a birth-death prior.
      • The rate prior is centred on the substitution rate that the tree and the root age imply.
      • Each clock model in --clock is run: independent (lognormal rates) and autocorrelated (geometric Brownian rates).
      • Each analysis runs as --chains independent chains with different seeds.
      • The same analysis is also run without the data, which gives the effective prior of every node: the joint effect of all calibrations and the tree prior, which can differ markedly from the bounds as written.
    3. LSD2 (least-squares dating, through IQ-TREE) on the same loci and topology, as a second, independent method. --method lsd2 uses it alone, which takes seconds.
    4. Cross-validation. Each calibration in turn is left out, and its node is estimated from the others. A calibration that the others cannot predict conflicts with them and is reported.

    Verdicts¶

    Every node gets a verdict:

    Verdict Meaning
    robust the chains agree and the age holds across schemes and clock models
    not converged effective sample size below 200, R-hat above 1.05, or chain means more than 5% apart
    scheme-sensitive calibration schemes disagree by more than 1.5-fold
    clock-sensitive the clock models disagree by more than 1.25-fold
    prior-driven the 95% posterior interval is more than 90% as wide as the prior's, so the sequences add little and the age is set by the calibrations

    Examples¶

    # from a BUSCO phylogeny, with the library alone (species from the fetch manifest)
mcscan3 phylo busco -o vert --pep ... --outgroup chicken
mcscan3 phylo date -o vert.date --from-busco vert --manifest genomes/manifest.tsv --cpus 16

# the library plus your own calibrations and schemes, both clocks
mcscan3 phylo date -o apic.date -c calibrations.tsv --library-confidence low --from-busco apic --cpus 16

# OrthoFinder species tree and single-copy orthogroups
mcscan3 phylo date -o of.date -c cal.tsv --from-orthofinder OrthoFinder/Results_Nov24 --cpus 16

# the topology of a synteny network tree, with BUSCO loci for the rates
mcscan3 phylo date -o net.date -c cal.tsv --from-network net --alignments apic.work --outgroup perkinsus_marinus

# a quick look with LSD2 only
mcscan3 phylo date -o quick -c cal.tsv --from-busco apic --method lsd2

    Outputs¶

    File Content
    OUT.dates.tsv every node under every scheme and clock: mean age, 95% HPD, prior interval, ESS, R-hat, LSD2 age, verdict
    OUT.<scheme>.<clock>.nwk dated tree (branch lengths in Myr; node labels mean[low-high])
    OUT.crossval.tsv each calibration left out: its bounds, the age predicted by the others, and whether they agree
    OUT.calibrations.tsv every calibration considered (library and yours): node, tips, bounds, used, replaced or skipped, and why
    OUT.taxonomy.tsv the NCBI lineage of every tip (reusable with --taxonomy)
    OUT.loci.tsv the loci used: rank, taxa, sites, concordance, root-to-tip CV, rate, partition
    OUT.chronogram.pdf the time tree with HPD bars coloured by verdict, calibrated nodes and geological eras
    OUT.sensitivity.pdf ages of the calibrated and deepest nodes under every scheme, clock and LSD2
    OUT.json settings and verdict counts
    OUT.work/ MCMCTree, codeml and LSD2 runs (in.BV, mcmc.txt of every chain); finished runs are reused

    Notes¶

    • Run length. The default (20,000 burn-in, 20,000 samples every 20 iterations) is enough for trees of a few hundred tips with the approximate likelihood. If a node is not converged, raise --nsample and --sampfreq.
    • Mitochondrial loci. Mitochondrial genes saturate at deep divergences and underestimate old ages, so date with nuclear loci where they exist.
    • Requirements. PAML (mcmctree, codeml) for MCMCTree and IQ-TREE 2 or 3 for LSD2. The LG matrix is bundled.

    Arguments¶

    Argument Description

    Options¶

    Option Description
    --prefix, -o Output prefix.
    --calibrations, -c Calibration table: scheme, name, taxa, min, max, source (Myr).
    --library Use MCscan3's calibration library, placed by NCBI Taxonomy.
    --library-confidence Least confidence of library calibrations: low, medium, high. (default: medium)
    --species Species of a tip, NAME=SPECIES
    --manifest Manifest of fetch genome/fetch taxon (species). (default: [])
    --taxonomy Tip lineages, TIPtaxon;taxon;... (no lookup).
    --from-busco Prefix of a phylo busco run (tree and loci).
    --from-orthofinder OrthoFinder results folder (rooted species tree, single-copy orthogroups).
    --alignments Aligned loci (FASTA files or a folder; sequence names = tree tips). (default: [])
    --tree Species tree to date (Newick; topology only).
    --from-network Prefix of a network tree run: date its topology.
    --rate-tree Tree with substitution branch lengths for the rate prior.
    --outgroup Root the tree on these tips. (default: [])
    --scheme Use only these calibration schemes (default: all). (default: [])
    --clock Clock models: independent, autocorrelated. (default: ['independent', 'autocorrelated'])
    --method mcmctree (Bayesian) or lsd2 (fast). (default: mcmctree)
    --loci Most clock-like loci used (0: all). (default: 100)
    --min-occupancy Least share of tips in a locus. (default: 0.5)
    --rate-partitions Partitions by locus rate. (default: 4)
    --chains Independent MCMC chains per analysis. (default: 2)
    --burnin MCMC burn-in iterations. (default: 20000)
    --sampfreq Sample every this many iterations. (default: 20)
    --nsample Samples per chain. (default: 20000)
    --no-prior-check Skip the run without data.
    --cross-validate Leave each calibration out: lsd2, mcmctree or none. (default: lsd2)
    --root-max Maximum root age (Myr) for schemes without one. (default: 0.0)
    --aligner For --from-orthofinder: famsa, mafft or muscle. (default: auto)
    --cpus Parallel jobs. (default: 1)
    --seed Random seed. (default: 12345)
    --format Figure format. (default: pdf)
    --dpi Dots per inch. (default: 300)

    Worked example¶

    Run on real data in the MCscan3 showcase (all commands on published genomes, in workflow order). The command line, console output, output files and figures below are those of that run.

    The BUSCO tree of fungi, insects and plants dated with MCMCTree and the calibration library (placed by NCBI Taxonomy from the species of the BUSCO run).

    mkdir -p families && mcscan3 phylo date --from-busco kingdoms/phylo/euk --loci 40 --burnin 4000 --nsample 4000 --chains 2 \
 --root-max 1800 --cross-validate none --cpus $CPU -o families/euk && ls families | grep euk

    run time 8 min 13 s · data real · showcase step phylo_date

    Variables: $CPU threads.

    Method and references¶

    New in MCscan3 (no JCVI equivalent). MCMCTree (Yang Z (2007) Mol Biol Evol 24:1586-1591) with the approximate likelihood (dos Reis M & Yang Z (2011) Mol Biol Evol 28:2161-2172), soft bounds (Yang Z & Rannala B (2006) Mol Biol Evol 23:212-226) and truncated-Cauchy minima (Inoue J, et al. (2010) Syst Biol 59:74-89); LSD2 (To TH, et al. (2016) Syst Biol 65:82-97) through IQ-TREE; clock-like loci ranked after SortaDate (Smith SA, et al. (2018) Syst Biol 67:1164-1176); calibration cross-validation after Near TJ, et al. (2005) Am Nat 165:137-146. Built-in calibration library placed through NCBI Taxonomy lineages; fossil ages after Benton MJ, et al. (2015) Palaeontol Electron 18.1.1FC and the sources listed in calibrations.tsv.
