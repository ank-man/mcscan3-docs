    mcscan3 phylo busco¶

    Phylogenomics from single-copy BUSCO genes.

    Group phylo · status new · new in MCscan3

    Synopsis¶

    mcscan3 phylo busco [OPTIONS]

    Description¶

    New in MCscan3, after the BUSCO_phylogenomics workflow (McGowan J & Fitzpatrick DA; reimplemented from its description) and BUSCO itself (Manni M et al. 2021, Mol Biol Evol 38:4647), made faster:

    1. markers: annotated proteomes (--pep NAME=FILE) are searched directly with the lineage's profile HMMs in one hmmsearch per genome (seconds instead of a BUSCO run); a protein is a match above the lineage's score cutoff (and counts only for the BUSCO it matches best), complete when its HMM coverage reaches the expected length minus two standard deviations, else fragmented; one complete match is single, more scoring at least 85% of the best are duplicated (weaker ones are paralogs, not copies). Finished BUSCO runs (--busco NAME=DIR) are read instead when given;
    2. loci: single-copy BUSCOs present in at least --occupancy of the genomes;
    3. alignment of each locus in parallel (FAMSA, MAFFT or MUSCLE; cached), trimming of gappy columns (--max-gap) natively, and loci shorter than --min-length dropped;
    4. supermatrix with a partition file (RAxML-NG and NEXUS formats), and trees: a concatenated maximum-likelihood tree (IQ-TREE's fast search with SH-aLRT support by default; --partitioned for a model per locus, --bootstrap for ultrafast bootstrap; or FastTree), gene trees (FastTree or IQ-TREE, in parallel) and their coalescent summary (ASTRAL);
    5. gene concordance factors of every branch, natively (Minh et al. 2020, Mol Biol Evol 37:2727), and the Robinson-Foulds distance between the concatenated and coalescent trees:
    mcscan3 phylo busco --pep scer=scer.pep --pep dmel=dmel.pep ... --manifest manifest.tsv \
    --outgroup ath -o tree/euk

    --lineage is a BUSCO dataset name (diptera_odb10; downloaded once into --lineage-dir) or folder (hmms/, scores_cutoff, lengths_cutoff). With auto (the default) the species of the genomes are looked up in NCBI Taxonomy and the most specific lineage holding them all is chosen (--odb sets the dataset version). Species come from --species NAME=SPECIES (a name or taxid), from fetch manifests (--manifest), or from genome names that are species names (Saccharomyces_cerevisiae). Writes <prefix>.status.tsv (status of every BUSCO in every genome), <prefix>.summary.tsv (BUSCO scores), <prefix>.loci.tsv, <prefix>.supermatrix.faa, <prefix>.partitions.{txt,nex}, <prefix>.concat.nwk, <prefix>.genetrees.nwk, <prefix>.astral.nwk, <prefix>.species.nwk (rooted, with gCF), <prefix>.concordance.tsv and the figures <prefix>.tree, <prefix>.matrix and <prefix>.compare. Intermediate files go to <prefix>.work/.

    Arguments¶

    Argument Description

    Options¶

    Option Description
    --prefix, -o Output prefix.
    --pep Proteome, NAME=FASTA (searched with the lineage HMMs; repeatable). (default: [])
    --busco Finished BUSCO/compleasm run, NAME=DIR (repeatable). (default: [])
    --lineage BUSCO dataset (diptera_odb10), its folder, or auto: chosen from NCBI Taxonomy. (default: auto)
    --species Species of a genome for --lineage auto, NAME=SPECIES
    --manifest Manifest of fetch genome/fetch taxon (species). (default: [])
    --odb OrthoDB version of the datasets for --lineage auto (10 or 12). (default: 10)
    --lineage-dir Where datasets are kept. (default: ~/.cache/mcscan3/busco)
    --occupancy Least share of genomes with a single-copy locus. (default: 0.8)
    --aligner famsa, mafft or muscle. (default: famsa)
    --max-gap Alignment columns with more gaps are trimmed. (default: 0.5)
    --min-length Loci shorter than this after trimming are dropped. (default: 50)
    --tree-tool Concatenated tree: auto, iqtree3, iqtree, FastTree. (default: auto)
    --gene-trees Gene trees: fasttree, iqtree or none. (default: fasttree)
    --model Model of the concatenated tree (IQ-TREE; MFP to select). (default: LG+G4)
    --bootstrap Ultrafast bootstrap replicates (full search; 0: none). (default: 0)
    --alrt SH-aLRT replicates for branch support (0: none). (default: 1000)
    --partitioned A model per locus (slower).
    --copy-ratio Least score of a second copy, share of the best. (default: 0.85)
    --outgroup Outgroup genome(s) (default: midpoint root). (default: [])
    --max-loci Use at most this many loci (0: all). (default: 0)
    --cpus Parallel jobs. (default: 1)
    --seed Random seed. (default: 1)
    --format Figure format. (default: pdf)
    --dpi Dots per inch. (default: 300)

    Worked example¶

    Run on real data in the MCscan3 showcase (all commands on published genomes, in workflow order). The command line, console output, output files and figures below are those of that run.

    A tree of fungi, insects and plants from single-copy BUSCO genes: the lineage (eukaryota_odb10) is chosen from NCBI Taxonomy, proteomes are searched directly, and the concatenated, gene and coalescent trees are compared with gene concordance. Duplicated BUSCOs show the whole-genome duplications of soybean and poplar.

    mkdir -p kingdoms/phylo && mcscan3 phylo busco --pep scer=kingdoms/fungi/scer.pep --pep klac=kingdoms/fungi/klac.pep --pep egos=kingdoms/fungi/egos.pep --pep kmar=kingdoms/fungi/kmar.pep --pep esin=kingdoms/fungi/esin.pep --pep dmel=kingdoms/insects/dmel.pep --pep dsim=kingdoms/insects/dsim.pep --pep dyak=kingdoms/insects/dyak.pep --pep dpse=kingdoms/insects/dpse.pep --pep agam=kingdoms/insects/agam.pep --pep ath=$R/data/ath.pep --pep grape=$R/data/grape.pep --pep peach=$R/data/peach.pep --pep poplar=$R/data/poplar.pep --pep soybean=$R/data/soybean.pep --pep amborella=$R/data/amborella.pep \
 --species 'scer=Saccharomyces cerevisiae' --species 'klac=Kluyveromyces lactis' --species 'egos=Eremothecium gossypii' --species 'kmar=Kluyveromyces marxianus' --species 'esin=Eremothecium sinecaudum' --species 'dmel=Drosophila melanogaster' --species 'dsim=Drosophila simulans' --species 'dyak=Drosophila yakuba' --species 'dpse=Drosophila pseudoobscura' --species 'agam=Anopheles gambiae' --species 'ath=Arabidopsis thaliana' --species 'grape=Vitis vinifera' --species 'peach=Prunus persica' --species 'poplar=Populus trichocarpa' --species 'soybean=Glycine max' --species 'amborella=Amborella trichopoda' \
 --outgroup scer --outgroup klac --outgroup egos --outgroup kmar --outgroup esin --cpus $CPU -o kingdoms/phylo/euk \
 && cat kingdoms/phylo/euk.summary.tsv

    run time 4 min 6 s · data real · showcase step kingdoms_phylo_busco

    Variables: $R the eudicot pipeline run (six genomes); $CPU threads.

    Method and references¶

    New in MCscan3 (no JCVI equivalent). After BUSCO_phylogenomics (McGowan J & Fitzpatrick DA, GitHub jamiemcg/BUSCO_phylogenomics) and BUSCO (Manni M, et al. (2021) Mol Biol Evol 38:4647-4654), reimplemented; lineage chosen from NCBI Taxonomy. Gene concordance factors after Minh BQ, et al. (2020) Mol Biol Evol 37:2727-2733. Uses HMMER, FAMSA/MAFFT/MUSCLE, IQ-TREE or FastTree and ASTRAL when installed.
