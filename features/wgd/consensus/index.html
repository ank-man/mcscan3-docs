    mcscan3 wgd consensus¶

    Whole-genome duplications placed on a species tree by consensus of four lines of evidence.

    Group wgd · status new · new in MCscan3

    Synopsis¶

    mcscan3 wgd consensus [OPTIONS] TREE

    Description¶

    New in MCscan3. Each line of evidence places WGD events on the branches of a rooted species tree on its own; an event is called where at least --min-support of them agree:

    1. block ratio (synteny depth). Every gene of a reference genome is covered by as many syntenic blocks of a genome as the genome has copies of that region relative to the reference. The ratio is the largest depth reached by at least --depth-share of the covered genes. Against an in-group reference, orthologous synteny is used when --soi-dir gives it (ortholog soi): paralogous blocks from events the reference shares would inflate the depth;
    2. quota ratio. The quota alignment (quota align) of the genome against the reference is solved for 1:1, 1:2, ... 1:--max-ratio. Anchors kept grow until the ratio reaches the true one, then stop growing (gain below --quota-gain).

    Copies of old events are too fragmented to count against a distant outgroup, so ratios are chained: the outgroup (--outgroup) has multiplier 1, and each genome is measured against its nearest genome (on the tree) of known multiplier that is not more duplicated than itself (multiplier = the reference's times the ratio), in passes until no multiplier changes.

    For both, multipliers are split into prime factors (x2 duplication, x3 triplication). The factors shared by every genome of a clade are placed on the clade's stem branch (parsimony), the rest on the branches below; 3. Ks. The peaks of each genome's syntenic block Ks (wgd blocks, mixture models of wgd mix) are placed against its speciations with a rate correction: along the branch to the split from a sister clade T, with an outgroup O, the genome's own Ks since the split is (Ks(S, T) + Ks(S, O) - Ks(T, O)) / 2, from orthologous anchors. A paralog peak older than twice that value predates the split (the reasoning of ksrates, Sensalari et al. 2022); 4. gene trees (PUG, McKain et al. 2016): the syntenic paralogs of each Ks peak (the anchors of the genome's self synteny, each block labelled by the mixture component of its Ks) are placed on the species tree by where they coalesce in the gene trees (wgd tree-map --paralogs; e.g. OrthoFinder's resolved gene trees, rooted on the outgroup's genes). This places each event by itself, independently of rates. A peak whose pairs fall in few gene trees (fewer than --min-tree-rate per pair: tandem arrays or a few large families) is not a genome-wide event and gets no Ks vote either. A placement split between two adjacent branches is flagged as possible allopolyploidy. Without syntenic paralogs, the duplications of the reconciled gene trees are counted instead (Tree2GD, Chen et al. 2023): a node with at least --burst times the median number is a burst.

    The number of WGDs called on a branch is the median count of the lines that saw events, with the Ks and gene-tree counts capped by the copy-number ratios when those saw events too (a mixture can split one peak in two; close peaks, less than 1.35 times apart, count once anyway).

    Writes <prefix>.evidence.tsv (per genome: multipliers and Ks peaks), <prefix>.tsv (per branch: the events of each line of evidence, the support and the call), <prefix>.events.tsv (per Ks peak: its branch by Ks and by gene trees, votes, share and status) and the figure <prefix>.<format>: the tree with the called WGDs (squares) and triplications (triangles) on their branches, coloured by support, and the evidence beside it:

    mcscan3 wgd consensus species.nwk --bed-dir data --anchors-dir synteny --cds-dir data \
    --blocks-dir wgd --outgroup amborella --gene-trees Resolved_Gene_Trees.txt -o wgd

    Arguments¶

    Argument Description
    TREE Rooted species tree (Newick).

    Options¶

    Option Description
    --bed-dir Directory of .bed. (default: .)
    --anchors-dir Directory of . anchors files. (default: .)
    --anchors-suffix Anchors file suffix. (default: lifted.anchors)
    --outgroup Genome without the events (default: root's single genome).
    --cds-dir Directory of .cds (for ortholog Ks).
    --blocks-dir Directory of .blocks.tsv (wgd blocks).
    --soi-dir Directory of ..soi.anchors (orthologous anchors).
    --gene-trees Gene trees (directory, or one per line; OrthoFinder).
    --gene-tree-sep Gene names are . (default: _)
    --depth-share Block ratio: share of genes at the depth. (default: 0.25)
    --max-ratio Quota ratio: largest 1:k tried. (default: 12)
    --quota-gain Quota ratio: smallest gain in anchors kept. (default: 0.08)
    --no-quota Skip the quota-ratio evidence.
    --burst Gene trees without syntenic paralogs: duplications per node over the median. (default: 2.0)
    --min-tree-rate Gene trees per paralog pair below which a Ks peak is not a WGD. (default: 0.05)
    --min-votes Gene trees needed to place a Ks peak. (default: 50)
    --min-support Lines of evidence needed to call a WGD. (default: 2)
    --ks-pairs Orthologous pairs per genome pair for Ks. (default: 300)
    --cpus CPUs for Ks. (default: 1)
    --prefix, -o Output prefix.
    --format Image format. (default: pdf)
    --dpi Dots per inch. (default: 300)

    Worked example¶

    Run on real data in the MCscan3 showcase (all commands on published genomes, in workflow order). The command line, console output, output files and figures below are those of that run.

    WGDs on the eudicot tree by consensus of block ratios, quota ratios, Ks and gene trees.

    mcscan3 wgd consensus $R/ancestral/species.nwk --bed-dir $R/data --anchors-dir $R/synteny --cds-dir $R/data --blocks-dir $R/wgd  --soi-dir $R/soi --outgroup amborella --gene-trees $R/orthology/orthofinder/Results_mcscan3/Resolved_Gene_Trees/Resolved_Gene_Trees.txt  --cpus $CPU -o wgd/consensus

    run time 44.2 s · data real · showcase step wgd_consensus

    Variables: $R the eudicot pipeline run (six genomes); $CPU threads.

    Method and references¶

    New in MCscan3 (no JCVI equivalent). MCscan3: synteny-depth and quota-alignment multipliers placed by parsimony, Ks peaks placed against rate-corrected speciation Ks (after ksrates, Sensalari et al. 2022), syntenic paralogs of each Ks peak placed by gene trees (after PUG, McKain et al. 2016) or gene-tree duplication bursts (after Tree2GD, Chen et al. 2023); events called where lines of evidence agree.
