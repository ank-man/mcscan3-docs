    mcscan3 karyotype events¶

    Trajectories from the ancestral karyotype to each genome, and shared events on the tree.

    Group karyotype · status new · new in MCscan3

    Synopsis¶

    mcscan3 karyotype events [OPTIONS] SEGMENTS...

    Description¶

    New in MCscan3, after the chromosome evolution models used with AKRUP and WGDI (Wang X et al. 2015, Plant Cell 27:1953 on grasses; Murat F et al. 2017, Genome Biol 18:200; Sun P et al. 2022, Mol Plant 15:1841). Each extant chromosome painted by karyotype map is read as a row of segments of ancestral chromosomes, each with its ancestral span (0 and 1 are the two ends, the telomeres, of the ancestral chromosome) and copy. Neighbouring segments meet at junctions, which are classified:

    • EEJ (end-to-end joining), EEJ:A-B: both segments meet with their ancestral ends (within --end-tol of 0 or 1). One chromosome is lost.
    • NCF (nested chromosome fusion), NCF:B>A: segments of B sit between two parts of the same copy of A, and the two parts of A are complementary (their spans hardly overlap): B was inserted into A, usually at the centromere. One chromosome is lost.
    • RCT (reciprocal translocation), RCT:A/B: two junctions of A and B, on different chromosomes, at least one end within the chromosome rather than at its end. The number of chromosomes stays.
    • TRA (other translocation), TRA:A-B: an unpaired junction within a chromosome. When both sides are broken inside, the exchange left one more chromosome (its other product split).
    • FIS (fission), FIS:A: a copy of A is split over more chromosomes than its translocations explain. One chromosome is gained.
    • WGD, WGD:x2: overlapping copies of the ancestral chromosomes (the median number over ancestral chromosomes) are whole-genome duplications, not fissions. The chromosome number expected from the events, ancestral chromosomes x copies + FIS - EEJ - NCF (plus one per TRA broken on both sides), is set beside the observed one as a check: a gap means events the segments do not resolve.

    Every event is a character, present or absent in each genome (<prefix>.characters.tsv). With a rooted species tree (--tree) the characters are mapped onto it by irreversible (Camin-Sokal) parsimony: an event can arise on several branches but never reverts, so independent whole-genome duplications and parallel fusions stay on their own branches and an ancestor carries only the events all its descendants share (--model dollo: one origin per event, losses elsewhere). The karyotype of every ancestor follows from the events it carries: which fusions it already had, and its chromosome number (ancestral chromosomes x WGD copies + FIS - EEJ - NCF, each event counted once; genomes keep their observed number). Shared EEJs and NCFs are how the ancestral karyotypes of grasses and eudicots were built (bottom-up); the events on the branches are the trajectory to each genome (top-down):

    mcscan3 karyotype events eudicot.segments.tsv --tree species.nwk -o eudicot

    Writes <prefix>.events.tsv (genome, type, event, chromosomes, detail), <prefix>.genomes.tsv (per genome: chromosomes, copies, counts of each event type, expected chromosomes), <prefix>.characters.tsv and, with --tree, <prefix>.nodes.tsv (per node: events gained on the branch above it, events carried, chromosome number) and the tree figure <prefix>.tree.<format>.

    Arguments¶

    Argument Description
    SEGMENTS Segments of karyotype map (.segments.tsv).

    Options¶

    Option Description
    --prefix, -o Output prefix.
    --tree Rooted species tree: events and ancestral karyotypes on it.
    --genomes Only these genomes (comma-separated).
    --end-tol Ancestral positions this close to 0 or 1 are ends. (default: 0.15)
    --min-genes Smaller segments are ignored (genes). (default: 50)
    --model irreversible (parallel gains, no reversals) or dollo (one gain). (default: irreversible)
    --format Image format. (default: pdf)
    --dpi Dots per inch. (default: 300)

    Worked example¶

    Run on real data in the MCscan3 showcase (all commands on published genomes, in workflow order). The command line, console output, output files and figures below are those of that run.

    Fusions, translocations and fissions from the ancestral karyotype; shared events on the tree.

    mcscan3 karyotype events karyotype/eudicots.segments.tsv --tree karyotype/species.nwk -o karyotype/events

    run time 0.5 s · data real · showcase step karyotype_events

    Method and references¶

    New in MCscan3 (no JCVI equivalent). Karyotype mapping after Sun P et al. (2022) WGDI: a user-friendly toolkit for evolutionary analyses of whole-genome duplications and ancestral karyotypes. Mol Plant 15:1841; chromosome events (end-to-end joining, nested chromosome fusion, translocation, fission) after Wang X et al. (2015) Plant Cell 27:1953 and Murat F et al. (2017) Genome Biol 18:200, as used by AKRUP; reimplemented. Events on the tree by Dollo parsimony (Farris JS 1977, Syst Zool 26:77).
